Forgotten Files

Forgotten Files is a first-person, narrative-driven puzzle thriller set within an abandoned 1920s manor hidden deep in the American countryside.

You play as FBI Detective McNeil, who has been assigned to investigate the mysterious disappearance of the home's former occupants. As you explore the decaying estate, you'll uncover forgotten notes, hidden clues, and long-buried secrets that slowly reveal the truth behind the house's tragic past.

But be careful...

Not everything in the darkness wants to stay forgotten.
